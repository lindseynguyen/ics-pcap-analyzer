# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers,
and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and
discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the
maintainers through GitHub (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private
contact options). Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially
and may remove comments or contributions, or block participants who violate
this code.
